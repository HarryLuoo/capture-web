Capture Shortcut

Open this page in Safari on the iPhone. Tap the button, then "Add Shortcut", and paste the inbox token when asked.

Add "Capture"opens the Shortcuts app

If that does nothing: download the file, then open the Files app → Downloads → tap "Capture".

What it does

  1. Run it by itself: a menu with Photo, Speak, Screen, Note.
  2. Share anything to it (a screenshot, a photo, a link, a post, text): it takes what you shared.
  3. It then offers one line for your words (empty is fine) and shows "Saved".

Where to put it

  1. Settings → Action Button → Shortcut → Capture.
  2. Lock Screen: long-press → Customize → a bottom control → Shortcut → Capture.
  3. Share sheet: it appears as "Capture"; move it up under "Edit Actions".
